## Q1: [Labor Law] What compliance requirements apply to cross-border e-commerce platforms?

**A**: Cross-border e-commerce compliance: 1) Customs registration and e-commerce enterprise filing; 2) Products must meet Chinese quality standards; 3) Tax compliance including customs duty, VAT, and consumption tax; 4) Consumer protection including 7-day return policy; 5) Data compliance under Data Security Law and PIPL.

**法律依据**: 《海关法》

**领域**: 跨境电商 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [New Company Law] For Overseas Chinese: What is the statute of limitations for labor arbitration?

**A**: According to Article 27 of the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law, the limitation period is one year from when the party knew or should have known their rights were infringed. For wage disputes during ongoing employment, the one-year limit does not apply; but after termination, claims must be filed within one year.

**法律依据**: 《社会保险法》

**领域**: 劳动法 | **语言**: English
