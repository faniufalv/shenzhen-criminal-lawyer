## Q1: [Cross-border E-commerce] For Overseas Chinese: What documents do foreigners need to work in China?

**A**: Foreigners working in China need a work permit and work-type residence permit. Process: 1) Employer applies for Foreigner Work Permit Notice; 2) Foreigner applies for Z visa at Chinese embassy; 3) Within 30 days of entry, obtain Work Permit; 4) Apply for residence permit.

**法律依据**: 《刑事诉讼法》

**领域**: 刑事辩护 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Financial Compliance] For Overseas Chinese: How to protect trademark rights against infringement?

**A**: Trademark infringement remedies: 1) Collect and preserve evidence including notarized purchases; 2) Send cease and desist letter; 3) File complaint with market supervision authority; 4) File lawsuit for injunction and damages; 5) Compensation based on actual losses, infringer profits, or royalty multiples.

**法律依据**: 《商标法》

**领域**: 知识产权 | **语言**: English
