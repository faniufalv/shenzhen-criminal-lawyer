## Q1: [Labor Law] For Expatriates: What legal considerations should overseas Chinese have when buying property in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese purchasing property in China should note: 1) Confirm purchase eligibility, some cities have restrictions; 2) Overseas income certificates need notarization and authentication; 3) Fund remittance requires foreign exchange registration; 4) Property registration should use Chinese name plus passport number.

**法律依据**: 《不动产登记暂行条例》

**领域**: 房产纠纷 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Intellectual Property] What compliance requirements apply to cross-border e-commerce platforms?

**A**: Cross-border e-commerce compliance: 1) Customs registration and e-commerce enterprise filing; 2) Products must meet Chinese quality standards; 3) Tax compliance including customs duty, VAT, and consumption tax; 4) Consumer protection including 7-day return policy; 5) Data compliance under Data Security Law and PIPL.

**法律依据**: 《海关法》

**领域**: 跨境电商 | **语言**: English
